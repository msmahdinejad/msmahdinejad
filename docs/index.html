01 About

I like projects that go all the way through.

I'm Mohammad Saleh, a computer engineering student at the University of Isfahan, on GitHub since December 2023. The Persian-language products and open-source tools further down this page are what I've been working on most recently.

Most of what I build has a model somewhere inside it, but the part that decides whether it holds up is everything around the model: retrieval that finds the right page, a queue that survives a crash, a confirmation step before anything irreversible.

I work in Persian and English, and so do the tools I make.

Cinewright

Teaches coding agents to make cinema-grade video, entirely from code.

  • 239 techniques in 17 families: 32 transitions, 50,000-particle morphs, a from-scratch 3D renderer, a sound engine.
  • Every frame is a pure function of time, so an agent can render one frame, look at it, fix one number and try again.
  • Persian and right-to-left text are first-class. No dependencies, no stock footage, no video model.
  • JavaScript
  • WebGL
  • Node.js
  • FFmpeg
  • Agent skills

Avorythm

Hear every voice, or just read it, in your language.

  • Live translated speech with original and translated captions, and four independent outputs: original audio, dub, source subtitles, translated subtitles.
  • A Chrome and Edge extension, on the Chrome Web Store, plus a desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux that also processes uploaded audio and video.
  • A synchronized player records a tab, dubs it and exports audio, subtitles and video. No analytics, no telemetry.

Newsroom

A news desk that runs on your own machine.

  • Collects from websites and feeds, GitHub, Reddit, Telegram and X, with a cursor, health check and cooldown for every source.
  • Evidence lineage lives in PostgreSQL. Reports go through a bounded multi-provider LLM router and arrive on Telegram.
  • English or Persian reports with Jalali dates. New sources and model routes stay off until you approve or validate them.

03 Approach

How I build agents.

Ground every claim.

Answers point at the page they came from. When the sources can't support an answer, the system says so instead of improvising.

Keep actions on a leash.

Tools have schemas. Irreversible steps wait for a yes. New sources and model routes stay switched off until someone has checked them.

Make long work survive.

Queues, checkpoints, retries and cancellation. Progress you can watch, and recovery when the process restarts.

Check the shape before the content.

Model output is parsed against a schema. A malformed tool call is repaired or retried, never trusted.
